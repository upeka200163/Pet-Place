Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Got questions about our clinical care, booking procedures, or shipping? Here are fast answers.

What should I prepare for my pet's first clinic visit?

Please bring any prior medical or vaccination records, a list of current foods and medications, and ensure dogs are leashed while cats are placed securely in a travel carrier. If your pet is anxious, let our staff know upon arrival so we can provide a quiet exam room.

How does your 24/7 emergency service work?

Our emergency trauma team is on-site 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. No advance appointment is required for severe emergencies. However, calling our emergency line (+94 70 876 5432) while en route allows our triage team to prepare the trauma suite and oxygen equipment before you arrive.

What are your shipping rates and delivery times for store orders?

We offer Free Standard Shipping on all product orders over $50.00! Orders placed before 2:00 PM are dispatched the same business day and typically arrive within 24-48 hours. Express same-day courier delivery is also available in select metropolitan zones.

What vaccinations are mandatory before pet boarding?

For the safety of all guests, dogs must be up-to-date on Rabies, DHPP (Distemper, Hepatitis, Parvovirus, Parainfluenza), and Bordetella (Kennel Cough). Cats require Rabies and FVRCP. Proof of flea/tick prevention within the last 30 days is also required.

Can I reschedule or cancel my appointment?

Yes! You can reschedule or cancel routine appointments free of charge with at least 12 hours advance notice by contacting our clinic via phone, email, or through your appointment confirmation voucher.
